How to study (start here)

  1. Open a course and go to the week (or chapter) your class is on.
  2. Click 📘 Start here: Guided lesson. It teaches the topic from zero, one step at a time: read a short explanation, open the lab it points to and do the listed tasks, answer the quick check, then click Next step.
  3. Want a video first? Each lesson lists YouTube searches for good explainer channels at the top.
  4. The last step sends you to the games, the quiz and the flashcards. Retake the quiz until you get 80% or more, and do the flashcards again the day before the exam.
  5. The next day, take the 🎯 Did I understand it? test. You get no hints and you rate how sure you are, so lucky guesses do not count. It tells you honestly whether you understood the week and which steps to redo. Pass it on two different days and the week counts as understood.
