How to study

  1. Open the lesson for the week your class is on.
  2. Go step by step. Read the big idea, study the picture, use the lab, answer the check.
  3. Practise at the end. Games, quiz and flashcards are in the last step. Aim for 80% on the quiz.
  4. Review every day. The daily review brings back the cards and questions you met, right before you would forget them.
  5. Test yourself tomorrow. No hints, and guesses do not count. Pass on two days and the week counts as understood.
