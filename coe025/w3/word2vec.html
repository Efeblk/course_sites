Static Embeddings Lab

An embedding table E stores one learned vector per vocabulary word; an ID just selects a row. How are those rows learned? Word2Vec predicts nearby words, GloVe fits global co-occurrence counts, FastText shares character fragments.

1 · Word2Vec: the text makes its own training examples

Click a word to make it the center.

CBOW — context → center
Skip-gram — center → each context word

No sentiment labels are needed: these targets come from the text itself. That's why Word2Vec can train on billions of words.

3 · Negative sampling

Instead of a softmax over the whole vocabulary: one observed pair (target 1) and a sampled pair (target 0), each a binary sigmoid decision.

4 · GloVe: count co-occurrences across the whole corpus

Rows: target words; columns: context words within the window. GloVe then fits vectors so that their dot products (plus biases) match the log of these counts, weighting frequent pairs. Compare: Word2Vec learns from one local window at a time.
