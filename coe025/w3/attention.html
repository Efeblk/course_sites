2 · Why √dₖ?

Random q and k with independent mean-0, variance-1 components: q·k has variance dₖ, so softmax gets sharper and sharper as dₖ grows. Dividing by √dₖ brings the variance back to 1.

3 · Masks are not [MASK]

A 1 means the query may attend to that key. A causal decoder blocks future keys so training can't peek at the text it must predict. BERT sees both sides (except padding). BERT's [MASK] token is input corruption — the masked position is still attended to.
