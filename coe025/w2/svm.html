Every point: margin and hinge loss

Signed margin m = y·f(x). Hinge loss = max(0, 1 − m): 0 when m ≥ 1, positive inside the street, above 1 on the wrong side. Objective J = ½||w||² + C·Σ hinge. Raw f(x) is a decision score, not a probability.
