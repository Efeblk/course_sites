Despite the name, it's a classifier: learn a weight per feature, take the weighted sum z = w·x + b, then squash it with the sigmoid p = σ(z) = 1/(1+e−z) to get P(positive).

2 · Learn: loss and one gradient step

Binary cross-entropy L = −[y ln p + (1−y) ln(1−p)]. By the chain rule the sigmoid's p(1−p) cancels and ∂L/∂wᵢ = (p − y)·xᵢ, ∂L/∂b = p − y. A feature with xᵢ = 0 gets no update — but the bias still learns.
