HMM & Viterbi Lab

Hidden tags generate visible words. A path's weight is start × emission × transition × emission … The defaults are the lecture's supplied "can fish" numbers (slides 26–29).

Parameters

N = NOUN, V = lexical VERB. Every row must sum to 1. An emission is P(word | tag), not P(tag | word).

Sentence:

1 · Enumerate every path

2 · Viterbi: one best partial path per ending tag

Each cell = max over predecessors of (previous best × transition), then × emission. The arrow is the backpointer.

3 · Training: count and normalize

With tagged sentences, transition probabilities are counts divided by the row total. The lecture example: N→N 3 times, N→V 7 times.
