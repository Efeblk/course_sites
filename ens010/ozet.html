0. Yazılı soruyu nasıl yazarsın

Paragraf ezberlemen gerekmiyor. Hoca hep "slayttaki şu kısmı yaz" diyor ve slaytları zaten madde madde. Yani istediği şey, slayttaki maddelerin hepsinin cevabında olması. Kelimesi kelimesine aynı cümle değil.

Örnek: photoacoustic için "you should completely describe these three steps" dedi. Burada completely = "üç adımın üçü de olsun, ortadaki adımı atlama". Hoca aynı derste süreci kendisi tek satırda özetledi: "it's just laser, acoustic wave out, that's it." Sadece bu satırı yazarsan eksik kalır, çünkü aradaki heat → expansion kısmı yok. Ama her adım için bir kısa satır yeterli. Aşağıdaki PAI kutusuna bak: üç satır.

Her yazılı soru için kalıp:
  1. Bir cümle tanım ("X is …").
  2. Numaralı maddeler: slayttaki adımlar ya da parçalar, her biri bir satır, içinde kalın yazılmış anahtar kelime.
  3. Mümkünse bir örnek (nanotube, gold NPs, MRI…).
Bu sayfadaki yeşil Kâğıda yaz kutuları tam bu kalıpta. Kalın kelimeler, puanın geldiği yerler; onları ezberle, cümleyi kendin kur. İngilizcen bozuk olsa da olur, anahtar kelime doğru olsun.

Lecture 2–3: temel tanımlar

1. Nanoscience vs nanotechnology

L2 · yazılı da çıkabilir, test de

Fark tek kelime: science = incelemek, technology = yapmak.

Kâğıda yaz
  • Nanoscience is the study of phenomena at the nano scale. It is theoretical (on paper, by computer).
  • Nanotechnology is creating and controlling materials, devices and systems atom by atom in the 1–100 nm range, using the new properties at that scale. It is practical (in the lab).
Tuzak
  • Şıkta create, control, atom by atom, lab geçiyorsa → nanotechnology.
  • "They are the same thing"

"If I ask you something in your mid-term exam, the differences between Nanoscience and nanotechnology."

2. What is a nanomaterial?

L3 most likely

Sihirli sayı 100 nm: bunun altında yüzey atomları çoğunluğa geçer ve özellikler değişir.

Kâğıda yaz
  • A nanomaterial is a material with at least one dimension in the range 1 to 100 nm.
  • Why 100 nm: below it the surface-to-volume ratio increases sharply, so the properties change. A 500 nm material still behaves like a bulk material.

Yan soru (L4): "What is nano size in mathematics?" → any size less than 1 micron (1000 nm). Yani 900 nm matematikte "nano"dur ama nanomaterial değildir.

Tuzak
  • "all three dimensions in 1–100 nm" (bu nanoparticle; nanomaterial için bir boyut yeter)
  • "a nanomaterial is 1–1000 nm" (1000 = matematikteki nano size)

"Be careful of this description. This is very important. If somebody asks you what is a nanomaterial, you should say a material with at least one dimension in the range 1 to 100 nanometers."

Lecture 4: boyuta göre sınıflandırma ve quantum

4. Classification by dimension: 0D, 1D, 2D

L4 most likely

Tek kural: isimdeki sayı = hâlâ büyük kalan (100 nm üstü) boyut sayısı. Nano olan boyut sayısı değil.

Kâğıda yaz

Nanomaterials are classified by how many dimensions are still outside the nano range:

  • 2D: one dimension in 1–100 nm → nano-film, coating, layer, sheet (graphene).
  • 1D: two dimensions in 1–100 nm → nanowire, nanorod, nanofiber, nanotube.
  • 0D: all three dimensions in 1–100 nm → nanoparticle.
  • No dimension below 100 nm → bulk material.

Yan soru: "What are nanoparticles?" → 0D nanomaterials: all three dimensions are in 1–100 nm, no free dimension left.

Tuzak
  • "A 2D nanomaterial has two nano dimensions" (tersi)
  • "A nanotube is 2D" (1D)
  • "3D nanomaterial" (yok; üç boyutu nano olan 0D)

"This slide and the whole information that I will talk in this slide could be a good option for your midterm exam… most probably I will ask you something from this slide."

5. Are all nanomaterials quantum materials?

L4 most likely

100 nm'deki sıçramanın bir benzeri 10 nm'de olur; oradan aşağısı quantum. 1–10, 1–100'ün içinde.

Kâğıda yaz
  • No. Quantum materials have at least one dimension in 1–10 nm.
  • 1–10 nm is inside 1–100 nm, so all quantum materials are nanomaterials.
  • But not all nanomaterials are quantum: a 50 nm material is nano but not quantum.
  • Quantum forms: quantum well (1 dimension), quantum wire (2), quantum dot (3).

"All quantum materials could be considered as nanomaterials, but not necessarily all nanomaterials could be considered as quantum materials. This is important to know."

Lecture 5: radyasyon ve invasive teknikler

6. Ionizing vs non-ionizing radiation

L5 · yazılı adayı

Tek soru: radyasyonun hücredeki bağları kıracak enerjisi var mı?

Ionizing (harmful)Enough energy to break the molecular bonds of our cells.
Examples: UV, X-rays, gamma rays.
Non-ionizing (safe)Not enough energy to ionize our cells.
Examples: radio/TV, mobile networks, IR, visible light, power lines, low-frequency EM fields.
Tuzak
  • "IR / visible light is ionizing" · "UV is non-ionizing"

"In your exam, if I ask you about ionizing and non-ionizing radiations, you should write these descriptions."

7. Invasive vs non-invasive imaging

L5 yazılı adayı · L6'da "which is non-invasive?" testi
InvasiveUses ionizing radiation.
CT (X-ray), PET and SPECT (gamma).
Non-invasiveUses non-ionizing radiation or only sound.
MRI (magnetic field + radio waves), ultrasound, optical imaging.

Optical imaging'e "minimally invasive" da denir, çünkü boya (dye) enjekte ediliyor.

"Invasive techniques are techniques that use ionizing radiations such as ultraviolet, UV, X-ray and gamma ray." · "Which of these techniques are non-invasive, MRI could be considered as one of those techniques."

Lecture 6: görüntülemede nanoparçacıklar

8. Roles of nanoparticles in MRI

L6 · yazılı adayı · hoca "two points" dedi

Sorun: klasik contrast agent gadolinium toksik ve vücutta her yere dağılıyor. NP ya ajanın yerine geçer, ya ajanı taşır.

Kâğıda yaz
  1. NPs as contrast agents: magnetic nanoparticles (e.g. SPIO, superparamagnetic iron oxide) improve the image quality. They must be biocompatible.
  2. NPs as carriers: we encapsulate existing agents like gadolinium in NPs, coat them with a polymer and target the site, so they don't spread everywhere → less toxicity and side effects.

In both cases the magnetic properties must be kept.

"If I ask you what are the applications of nanoparticles or roles of nanoparticles in MRI? You can consider two points."

9. Photoacoustic imaging (PAI): procedure, advantages, agents

L6 · yazılı adayı

Hikâye: ışık girer → ısınır → genleşir → ses çıkar. Adındaki "photo" = giren ışık, "acoustic" = çıkan ses.

Kâğıda yaz (üç adım, her biri bir satır)
  1. The tissue is illuminated with a laser pulse.
  2. The light is absorbed, electrons are excited, they relax back to the ground state and the energy is released as heat.
  3. The heated area expands (thermal expansion) and produces a thermoacoustic (sound) wave, detected by an ultrasound transducer.

Advantages: anatomical, functional and molecular contrast in a single modality; reaches several centimeters deep.

Contrast agents: gold NPs, silver NPs, SWNT: they absorb light strongly and convert light to sound efficiently.

Bu kutudaki her şey slayt 10'da var, fazlası yok. Hocanın "completely" demesi: 2. adımdaki electrons → heat ve 3. adımdaki expansion → wave → transducer eksik olmasın.

Tuzak
  • "PAI is the same as ultrasound": PAI'de giren ışık, ultrasound'da giren de çıkan da ses.
  • "The detected signal in PAI is light": algılanan ses.

"If I ask you what is the procedure of photoacoustic, you should completely describe these three steps. If I ask you about the advantages, here it is. And if I ask you about the contrast agents, these are the contrast agents."

10. How do micro/nano-bubbles work in ultrasound?

L6 · yazılı adayı

Baloncuklar patlamaz, ses dalgasında şişip büzülür; bu titreşim echo'yu güçlendirir.

Kâğıda yaz
  • Bubbles have a gas core and a shell (protein, lipid or polymer). They are the only clinically used ultrasound contrast agent and stay intravascular.
  • In ultrasound waves they undergo volumetric oscillation: they expand and contract (they don't burst).
  • This gives a strong nonlinear acoustic response and high backscatter → higher signal-to-noise ratio → sharp contrast.
Tuzak
  • "Bubbles work by bursting" · "Ultrasound is invasive"

"I have written in this specifically because if I ask you, you know how to respond me."

Lecture 7: biosensors (en önemli ders)

Son derste "en çok neye odaklanalım?" sorusuna cevap: "The transducers are important. The performance slide is very important. These are the parts that are mostly examinable." Yani 12 ve 13.

11. Biosensor parts: analyte, receptor, transducer

L7 · test ağırlıklı

analyte → receptor (biocatalyst) → transducer → readable signal

Analyte = aradığımız şey (örn. antigen). Receptor = ona yapışan parça. Transducer = olanı okunabilir sinyale çeviren parça.

Kâğıda yaz
  • A biosensor is a device that detects an analyte (a sign of a disease) to diagnose it.
  • The receptor (biocatalyst) binds the analyte by lock and key: an enzyme, antibody, nucleic acid or cell.
  • The transducer converts this biological signal into a readable, quantifiable signal (electrochemical, optical, thermal, piezoelectric).
Tuzak
  • "An antigen is a receptor": antigen = analyte, antibody = receptor.
  • "A thermal transducer is a receptor"

"If I ask you in your midterm next week if the antigens could be considered as receptor. Of course they are not. Antigens are analytes."

12. Amperometric, calorimetric, optical biosensors

L7 most likely · tanım yazdırabilir ya da test

Biosensor adını neyi ölçtüğünden alır: ampere = akım, calorie = ısı, optic = ışık.

Kâğıda yaz
  • Amperometric: measures the current produced in proportion to the bioreaction (more analyte → more current).
  • Calorimetric (thermal): measures the heat produced or absorbed by the bioreaction.
  • Optical: measures the change in light intensity when the bioreaction happens.

Tablonun geri kalanı (test şıkkı olabilir): potentiometric = potential · piezo-electric = mass · thermistor = temperature · fluorescence = intensity, colour.

Tuzak
  • "Amperometric measures heat" (hocanın kendi örneği) · "Potentiometric measures current"

"I expect you, specifically for your midterm, to memorize these three different transducers… I may ask you to write the definition of each of them, or … in a multiple choice question, which of them is correct."

13. Performance parameters of a biosensor

L7 most likely · tanım eşleştirme

Hoca tanımları yer değiştirip soruyor ("selectivity is how sharply…" → yanlış). Her tanımı doğru isimle eşle.

ParameterDefinition (write this)Akılda tut

Sensitivityhow sharply the signal changes with the concentrationhassas tepki

Limit of detectionthe minimum concentration it can detect (lower = earlier diagnosis)minimum!

Tuzak
  • "Selectivity = how sharply the signal changes" (bu sensitivity)
  • "Limit of detection = the maximum concentration" (minimum)
  • "Accuracy = maintaining the signal over time" (bu durability)

"I may ask you, for example, if the selectivity is how sharply the signal change. Of course it is not. This is the definition for sensitivity. I may tell you that the limit of detection is the maximum concentration. Of course it is not."

L2–L3: nano, NBIC, neden boyut önemli
  • Nano = 10-9 (Greek nanos = dwarf). 1 nm = 10-9 m = 10 Å.
  • NBIC: Nano, Bio, Info, Cogno; ortak amaç enhancing human performance.
  • S-curve: nanotechnology şu an rapid growth phase'in başında.
  • Boyut küçülünce surface-to-volume ratio artar. Catalytic activity artar (activation energy düşer). Melting point düşer (yüzey atomları az bağlı, ilk onlar erir).
  • Carbon nanotube küçük çapta bile iletir → çipler küçüldü. NPs blood-brain barrier'dan geçebilir; sağlık uygulamaları "the most important".
L5: önleme ve erken teşhis
  • Sağlıkta üç alan: prevention, diagnosis, treatment.
  • Prevention: antibacterial NPs (Ag, ZnO, TiO2) maske, eldiven, PPE, sunscreen içinde. Uzun vadeli etkisi bilinmiyor → certificate, precaution.
  • Early diagnosis neden önemli: en iyi tedaviyi seçmek, tedavinin etkisini artırmak, kaynak israfını önlemek.
  • Diagnosis türleri: imaging, biosensors, wearable sensors. Tek bir imaging tekniği yetmez: her birinin artısı eksisi var.
  • Optical imaging: NIR + dye (ICG); zayıf yanı derine inmemesi. Nano probes: gold NPs, upconversion NPs, quantum dots.

L7: in vivo / ex vivo, nanomalzeme nerede
  • In vivo = vücut içi (implantable, monitoring). Ex vivo = vücut dışı (glucose sensor, wearable).
  • Nanomalzemeler (nanofibers, functionalized NPs, graphene) receptor'ları stabilize eder, transducer'ı verimli ve küçük yapar → wearable, implantable.
