ENS010 — study videos

Study videos

One narrated, illustrated video per lecture, in English like the course and the exam. Every idea is drawn and moves with the voice, and the exam traps are shown being tried and rejected. About 20 minutes in total.

The video files are rendered locally with Remotion (tools/motion/, recipe in its README) into materials/ENS010/video/. They are not in the GitHub repo (too large), so they play only on this computer. If a player stays black, render them once with tools/motion/render.py.
