EM: Soft Clustering

Like k-means, but soft: each element belongs to every cluster with some probability. Clusters are Gaussians (μᵢ, σᵢ²). The E-step computes those probabilities; the M-step re-fits each Gaussian to the elements, weighted by them.

Curves: πᵢ·N(x | μᵢ, σᵢ²) for each cluster; dashed = their sum. Each bar under the axis is one element, split by its probabilities. Click near an element to inspect it.

The two steps

StepWhat it doesFormula (1-D)
E (expectation)For each cluster, how much does each element seem to belong to it?γᵢᵗ = πᵢ N(xᵗ | μᵢ, σᵢ²) / Σⱼ πⱼ N(xᵗ | μⱼ, σⱼ²)
M (maximization)Adjust each (μᵢ, σᵢ²) to fit the elements, weighted by γμᵢ = Σₜ γᵢᵗxᵗ / Σₜ γᵢᵗ, σᵢ² = Σₜ γᵢᵗ(xᵗ − μᵢ)² / Σₜ γᵢᵗ, πᵢ = Σₜ γᵢᵗ / N

Replace γ by 0/1 (nearest center wins) and drop σ, and the M-step becomes exactly k-means' update mᵢ = Σₜ bᵢᵗxᵗ / Σₜ bᵢᵗ.
