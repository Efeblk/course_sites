Hierarchical Clustering

Distances between the current groups

Minkowski: d(xʳ, xˢ) = [Σⱼ |xⱼʳ − xⱼˢ|ᵖ]^(1/p). Single-link: d(Gᵢ, Gⱼ) = min over pairs. Complete-link: max over pairs. Average-link: mean over pairs. Centroid: distance between the group means.
