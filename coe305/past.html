Question 1: Decision trees ve sınıflandırıcılar

A1.I Saf düğümün entropy'si

3 puan · Vize notlarıyla örtüşüyor: Entropy
In a specific node of the decision tree, there are 20 instances of Class A but no instances of other classes. Calculate the entropy of the target variable at this node.
  1. 1.0
  2. 2.0
  3. 0.918
  4. 0.0

Hepsi aynı sınıfsa düğüm pure (saf). Hesap yapmana gerek yok: saf düğümde belirsizlik yok, entropy sıfır. 1.0 ise tam tersi uç: iki sınıf yarı yarıya ("fifty-fifty is the worst").

  • d. 0.0
  • p(A) = 20/20 = 1 → Entropy = −1 · log₂(1) = 0. All instances belong to one class, so the node is pure: no impurity, it becomes a leaf.

  3. criterion

A1.IV 8 pozitif, 4 negatif: entropy

7 puan · Vize notlarıyla örtüşüyor: Entropy · Log hesabı ek pratik
You are building a decision tree for a binary classification problem. The dataset at a node contains the following class distribution: 8 positive samples, 4 negative samples. Calculate the entropy of this node. Show your work.

Üç adım: (1) oranları bul, (2) her oran için p · log₂(p), (3) topla ve eksiyle çarp. Kontrol: sonuç 0 ile 1 arasında olmalı; düğüm yarı yarıya değil, saf da değil, yani 1'e yakın ama 1'den küçük.

  1. N = 8 + 4 = 12 · p₊ = 8/12 = 2/3 ≈ 0.667 · p₋ = 4/12 = 1/3 ≈ 0.333
  2. Entropy = −p₊ log₂ p₊ − p₋ log₂ p₋
  3. log₂(2/3) ≈ −0.585 · log₂(1/3) ≈ −1.585
  4. = 0.667 × 0.585 + 0.333 × 1.585 = 0.390 + 0.528 ≈ 0.918
  5. Meaning: the node is impure but not the worst case (a 50/50 node gives 1.0).

A2.IV Random Forest'ı SVM ve KNN'den ayıran ne

5 puan · Vize listesinde yok; soru tarzı için
Discuss how Random Forest algorithm stands unique when compared with SVM, KNN algorithms.

Ana fikir: Random Forest tek model değil, birçok karar ağacının oylaması. SVM tek bir sınır çizer, KNN hiç model kurmaz, komşulara bakar.

  • Random Forest: an ensemble of many decision trees; each tree is trained on a bootstrap sample and a random subset of features; the forest predicts by majority vote (or average).
  • Averaging many trees reduces the overfitting of a single deep tree; it gives feature importance; trees split on thresholds, so no feature scaling is needed.
  • SVM: one model that finds the maximum-margin boundary; needs scaling and a kernel for non-linear data.
  • KNN: a lazy, nonparametric learner: stores the training data and classifies by the nearest neighbours' distance; slow at prediction and sensitive to scaling.

A2.V SVM: kernel, soft margin, hard margin

6 puan · Vize listesinde yok; soru tarzı için
Write about kernels, soft margin, hard margin in Support Vector Machine.
  • SVM finds the separating hyperplane with the largest margin; the closest points are the support vectors.
  • Hard margin: no point may be misclassified or inside the margin; works only if the data is linearly separable; very sensitive to outliers.
  • Soft margin: allows some points inside the margin or on the wrong side (slack); the parameter C sets the trade-off: large C = few errors, narrow margin; small C = wider margin, more tolerance.
  • Kernel: maps the data into a higher-dimensional space where it becomes linearly separable, without computing the mapping (kernel trick). Examples: linear, polynomial, RBF.

A3.V Linear regression: homoscedasticity ve residual normality

5 puan · Vize listesinde yok; soru tarzı için
In the context of Linear Regression, explain the assumptions of homoscedasticity and normality of residuals, and why they are important.

Önce residual'ı tanımla (gerçek − tahmin). Sonra iki varsayımı birer cümleyle, nasıl kontrol edildiğini ve bozulursa ne olduğunu yaz.

  • Residual = actual − predicted value.
  • Homoscedasticity: the residuals have constant variance for all values of x. Check: residual plot shows an even band, not a funnel. Violation = heteroscedasticity.
  • Normality of residuals: residuals are roughly normally distributed around 0. Check: histogram or Q-Q plot.
  • Why: standard errors, confidence intervals and significance tests of the coefficients rely on them; if they fail, these become unreliable even if predictions look fine.

If RMSE = 0, what does it indicate about the model, and what would the corresponding R² (coefficient of determination) value be?

  2. The model has perfect predictions with no errors, and R² = 1.
  3. The model is underfitting the data, and R² = 0.

A5.IV Tomek link

5 puan · Vize listesinde yok; soru tarzı için
What is the purpose of Tomek link? How does it work?

Amaç (dengesiz veriyi temizlemek) + mekanizma (farklı sınıftan, birbirinin en yakın komşusu olan çift) + sonuç (sınır netleşir).

  • Purpose: an undersampling / cleaning method for imbalanced data; removes noisy and overlapping points near the class boundary.
  • How: a Tomek link is a pair of points from different classes that are each other's nearest neighbours. For every link, remove the majority-class point (or both points).
  • Result: the classes overlap less, the decision boundary becomes clearer, and the majority class shrinks a little.

A5.V One-hot encoding ve dummy variable trap

6 puan · Vize listesinde yok; soru tarzı için
In a dataset with 5 categorical variables (A, B, C, D, E), all of which are non-numeric, demonstrate how one-hot encoding can be applied to convert these variables into numerical format. Explain the concept of the dummy variable trap and how to address it.

Bir değişkenle küçük bir örnek göster, sonra "A–E'nin her birine aynısı" de. Trap: sütunların toplamı hep 1, yani biri diğerlerinden bulunabilir.

  • One-hot encoding: a variable with k categories becomes k binary (0/1) columns; each row has a single 1. Example, A ∈ {red, green, blue}:
AA_redA_greenA_blue
red100
green010
blue001
  • Repeat for B, C, D, E (e.g. pd.get_dummies(df, columns=['A','B','C','D','E'])).
  • Dummy variable trap: the k columns of one variable always add up to 1, so one column can be predicted from the others → perfect multicollinearity; regression coefficients become unstable.
  • Fix: keep k − 1 columns per variable (drop one: drop_first=True); the dropped category is the baseline (all zeros).

B3.B Overfit ve underfit: nedenleri, örnekleri

10 puan · Vize listesinde yok; soru tarzı için
Explain what causes a machine learning model to overfit and underfit with suitable examples.

Slayttaki iki tanımla başla: model (H) gerçek fonksiyondan daha karmaşık → overfit, daha basit → underfit. Sonra her birine neden, örnek, belirti, çare. Örnek olarak ağaç iyi: budanmamış derin ağaç = overfit.

  • Overfitting: the hypothesis H is more complex than the true function; the model learns the noise of the training data. Sign: high training accuracy, low test accuracy.
  • Causes: too complex a model, too little training data, noisy data. Examples: a very deep, unpruned decision tree with one leaf per training instance; a high-degree polynomial that passes through every training point.
  • Underfitting: H is less complex than the true function; the model misses the pattern. Sign: low accuracy on both training and test data.
  • Causes: too simple a model, too few or weak features. Example: a straight line fitted to curved data; a tree stopped at depth 1.
  • Remedies: overfit → pruning, simpler model, more data, cross-validation to choose complexity; underfit → more complex model, better features. Goal: good generalization (performance on new data).
