Regression & Overfitting

Data comes from rᵗ = f(xᵗ) + ε. We pick a model g from H — here polynomials of degree k — and minimize E(g | X) = (1/N) Σ [rᵗ − g(xᵗ)]² on the training set. The question is how well g does on data it has not seen.
