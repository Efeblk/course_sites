Family Car Hypothesis Lab

Is car x a family car? Inputs x₁ = price, x₂ = engine power. Our hypothesis class H is axis-aligned rectangles: h(x) = 1 inside, 0 outside. The error E(h | X) = Σₜ 1(h(xᵗ) ≠ rᵗ) counts mistakes.

Drag on the plot to draw your rectangle h. ⊕ = family car (r = 1), ⊖ = not (r = 0). Mistakes are circled.
