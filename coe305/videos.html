COE305 — explainer videos

Six short episodes for the midterm

The lecturer's topic list as six animated episodes of two to three minutes each (about 15 minutes in total), in English like the exam. Each one starts from a puzzle, uses the slides' own examples (credit scoring, the students' features, Play Tennis, the AND unit, slide 17's one weight) and ends with a short "for the exam" card. No formulas to memorize; the point is what each idea does and why we use it.

The video files are rendered on this computer with Remotion (tools/video/, see its README) into materials/COE305/video/. They are committed with the repo, so any checkout of course_sites plays them.
