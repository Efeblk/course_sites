Multithreading Models

User threads are managed by a library; kernel threads are what the OS actually schedules on cores. The model is how the two are mapped (§4.3). Block a thread on I/O and see who can still run.

How Linux does it

Linux is one-to-one. pthread_create() calls clone(fn, stack, CLONE_VM | CLONE_FILES | CLONE_SIGHAND | CLONE_THREAD, …): a new task (task_struct) that shares the address space (mm_struct), files and signal handlers with its parent. Linux threads are not special "lightweight processes" — they are tasks that happen to share memory.
