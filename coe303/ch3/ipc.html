2 · What if we use all N slots with a counter?

Slides 61–62. The producer does counter++ and the consumer counter--. Each is three machine instructions. Start: counter = 5. One item is produced and one consumed, so the answer must be 5. You are the CPU scheduler — choose who runs next.
