COE303 — explainer videos

Eight short episodes for the midterm

Chapters 1–6 as eight animated episodes of about two and a half minutes each (about 20 minutes in total), in English like the exam. The same cast runs through the series: the processes are little characters that queue, hop onto the CPU, wait and get preempted. The worked examples are the ones on the slides: 24, 3, 3 for FCFS and Round Robin, count = 5 for the race.

The video files are rendered on this computer with Remotion (tools/video/, see its README) into materials/COE303/video/. They are committed with the repo, so any checkout of course_sites plays them.
