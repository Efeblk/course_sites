Critical Section Lab

The producer runs count++, the consumer runs count--, and count starts at 5 (the producer–consumer deck). The correct final value is 5. You are the scheduler: each click runs one line of one process.

Pick a process to run.

What to notice

  • No protection: both processes can be between register = count and count = register at the same time. That is two processes in the critical section, so mutual exclusion fails and the result can be 4 or 6.
  • Mutex lock: acquire() refuses while the other process holds the lock. The waiting process does not advance; with a spinlock it would keep looping here (busy waiting).
  • Peterson: when both set their flag, the process that wrote turn last waits, because it gave the turn away.
