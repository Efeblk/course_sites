Remember (slides 12–14): PPV and NPV depend strongly on prevalence, so the same model behaves differently in different settings. Internal validation tests performance inside the development data; external validation tests another site or time period. A model with strong development results is not automatically ready for clinical use — reassess after deployment and whenever data or workflow change.
