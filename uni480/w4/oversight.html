Human Oversight Trainer

Augmented intelligence: AI extends human capability, and the human is not merely a rubber stamp (slide 15). For each case, read the record and the AI output, then accept it or override / escalate. Some outputs are right. Some are wrong because of stale data, a wrong-patient record, a unit error or missing context.

Error types (slide 16). Commission error: following an incorrect recommendation. Omission error: failing to act because the system did not flag a problem. Under-trust: ignoring useful support. The aim is calibrated trust — not "always trust" and not "never trust".
