Ward Monitoring Agent

An AI agent perceives its environment through sensors and acts on it through actuators to reach a goal (slides 9–10). Here the agent watches 10 patients on a ward for 24 hours and pages the nurse when it thinks someone is deteriorating. You design it, then judge it with its performance measure.
