How to judge a sentence

  • Supported — the record says this.
  • Contradicts the record — the record says something different.
  • Unsupported (hallucination) — a fabricated fact, test, reference or policy that is not in the record (slide 22).
  • Misleading / missing context — partly true, but it ignores a patient-specific detail or uncertainty.

The safer pattern (slide 22)

Constrain the task → use trusted sources → request uncertainty → verify key claims → document human review.

In this draft the most dangerous errors are the allergy statement, the wrong antibiotic and "all results normal" while a culture is still pending — a patient or GP acting on them could be harmed. Automation bias makes them easy to miss, because the rest of the text is polished and correct. The human role shifts toward defining the task, checking evidence and taking responsibility (slide 20).
