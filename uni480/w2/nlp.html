Why it matters

A naive extractor turns "denies chest pain" into a chest-pain record. Downstream, a decision-support rule may then fire, a cohort for research may include the wrong patient, or a billing code may be added. NLP errors can be subtle: omissions, wrong negation, wrong temporality or unsupported inferences (slide 13).

Even the smarter rule system fails on the two "tricky" notes. Real clinical NLP uses trained models (up to transformer language models), and its output still needs checking.
